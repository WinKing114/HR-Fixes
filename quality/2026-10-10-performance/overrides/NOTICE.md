# Hollow Realm intelligent-gem data override

This corrected definition follows Arcane Convergence 1.0.0bugfix by Rain, whose installed Forge metadata declares the MIT license. Its attribute, operation, numeric values, weight and quality are preserved; the existing Ars Nouveau mana attribute ID and the unregistered spellbook category are corrected.

The original mod and its JAR are not distributed here. HR Fixes' MIT license covers the compatibility changes. Retain this attribution when distributing the override with Hollow Realm.

Install `kubejs/data/irons_spellbooks/gems/intelligent.json` under the instance directory. This companion data applies to the audited Hollow Realm combination: Apotheosis 7.4.8, Arcane Convergence 1.0.0bugfix and Ars Nouveau 4.12.7. It is separate from the HR Fixes JAR and must be reviewed when these definitions change.
